===================================================

-----------------

One normalized-W source, independent of old F0 and derivative literals
---------------------------------------------------------------------
For each positive edge e=(i,j), use the OLD parent DBM whole-span domain
[l_e/SC,u_e/SC], SC=32768. Its deterministic anchor is the whole-span
midpoint p_e=(l_e+u_e)/(2SC), and R_e=(u_e-l_e)/(2SC)>0. There is no
selection among anchors. The whole interval contains the anchor and the
complete segment between it and every permitted real span value.

Normalized actual-W integer Taylor data contain24 terms of degree23,
enclosing w^(k)(n)/k! for k=0,...,23. At each rational p_e, Taylor is
applied SEPARATELY to w^(j) for j=0,...,6; the additional remainder is

    M24*abs(p_e-n)^(24-j)/(24-j)!, M24=(44/7)^24.

This does not differentiate an inequality about an unknown remainder.
All used nearest integer anchors are in1,...,32 and at distance<=1/2.
Midpoint/half-width of the resulting rational intervals give mu_j,eps_j.
In particular j=0 is included. The NEW energy does not borrow the old
common F0 packet, and the NEW derivative envelopes do not read the old
cover_newjet w-prime literal table. Old geometry and normalized variables
are the only fields taken from the quartic model for this construction.

A complete-span M7 bounds abs(w^(7)). On each old1/64 grid cell, the
normalized-W polynomial is differentiated7 times, giving17 terms of
degree16, with coefficient factor k!/(k-7)!. The Taylor remainder is

    M24*(1/2)^17/17!.

Every selected cell's rational lower/upper bounds and outward1e-15
absolute bound are stored once. The whole-span M7 is their maximum.
This matches q=7,d=16, q+d+1=24. It is not q=6 or an18th-power remainder.
There is no new transcendental table or actual scalar sampling position.

The normalized-W table identity and generic actual order24 derivative
APIs are already available, but the particular point/grid packets still
need their concrete finite checks or exact-rational interval soundness
consumer in Lean. Exact rational Horner is not silently identified with
FI-Q30 rounded endpoints. No new concrete analytic instance is claimed
Lean-checked here; this is an auditable static packet with explicit
actual-enclosure premises.

Correct energy and derivative payments
---------------------------------------
For d=s_e(y)-p_e and abs(d)<=R, define

    T6(d)=sum(j=0,...,6) mu_j*d^j/j!,
    A6(d)=eps0+eps1*R
           +(eps2/2+eps3*R/6)*d^2
           +(eps4/24+eps5*R/120)*d^4
           +(eps6/720+M7*R/5040)*d^6.

The actual energy enclosure is T6-A6<=w(s)<=T6+A6. All odd coefficient
errors are paid with abs(d)^(2k+1)<=R*d^(2k). The seventh energy remainder
uses7!=5040 and is bounded by M7*R*d^6/5040. The absolute lower energy is

    L6=pressure+sum_e a_e*(T6_e-A6_e).

Its use of mu0/eps0 avoids any inheritance of the old F0 interval. All
nonzero ledger weights are checked positive and pressure is the exact
same nine-gap linear form.

For w-prime, SEPARATELY apply its degree-five Taylor enclosure:

    U5(d)=sum(j=1,...,6) mu_j*d^(j-1)/(j-1)!,
    E6(d)=eps1+eps2*R
           +(eps3/2+eps4*R/6)*d^2
           +(eps5/24+eps6*R/120)*d^4
           +M7*d^6/720.

Then abs(w'(s)-U5(d))<=E6(d). This remainder uses6!=720, not7!.
E6 is not obtained by differentiating A6; differentiating an absolute
error bound would be invalid. Only the CENTRAL coefficient identity
U5[k]=(k+1)*T6[k+1] is checked as a useful arithmetic consistency test.

For each original coordinate r let

    P5_r=b_r+sum_(e contains r) a_e*U5_e,
    Err6_r=sum_(e contains r) a_e*E6_e.

All nine adjacent original lower endpoints are strictly above SEP.
Thus an actual relevant global minimum in this cell has gradient_r=0
and abs(P5_r)<=Err6_r. With each normalized abs(y_s)<=1, the necessary
scalar constraints are

    L6<=c,
    Err6_r+sign*P5_r>=0,
    Err6_r+sign*y_s*P5_r>=0, sign=-1,+1.

There are1+9*10*2=181 rows. The sign is Err6+/-P5, NEVER P5-Err6>=0.
Only P5 is multiplied by the test monomial; the error is NOT multiplied.
The new energy condition is unsoftened: the diagnostic energy slack
column5004 is absent from EVERY new row. For actual F<c the stronger
L6<c holds; the closed L6<=c is a valid necessary relaxation.

All rows have degree<=6 and are scalar/M0 constraints. A new Err6+/-P5
multiplier times an M1 square basis would require degree8 moments; no
such invalid localizer is added to the5005-monomial layout. The old14383
rows and73 PSD blocks are retained, giving14564 scalar rows and still73
PSD blocks if the new packet is later assembled with that model.

Full shared-variable factored representation
---------------------------------------------
All spans use the SAME nine y coordinates and the SAME canonical5005
monomials through degree six. The first715 monomials remain the old
degree<=4 prefix. MomentID0 is fixed1, positive momentID k means column
k-1, and separate energy slack column5004 is never a moment. No
independent edge variables or edge moments are introduced.

Each edge stores its exact affine d polynomial and all powers d^0,...,d^6
as canonical moment-ID coefficient lists. There are287 powers with41794
stored coefficients, each stored once. Univariate T6,A6,L6,U5,E6
coefficient lists multiply those powers; the positive edge weight is
also stored once. Incident-edge lists recover each P5_r and Err6_r
exactly. The exact pressure polynomial and pressure derivatives b_r are
stored explicitly.

The2501 shift plans cover every unshifted power and every product of a
power d^k, k<=5, with each of the nine y coordinates. A plan is

    [spanPowerID,testMonomialID,targetOffset,termCount].

Test monomial IDs0,...,9 mean1,y0,...,y8. The binary target stream is
little-endian uint16 canonical moment IDs. It has206278 entries. For
every coefficient monomial alpha, its target is alpha+test; injectivity
and maximum degree6 are checked. Degree-six powers have ONLY test0,
because they are never multiplied by y in the new constraints.

This representation determines each scalar row as a complete exact
rational linear form, including all cross-coordinate y_s*d^k moments.
It does not discard cross terms or replace them with independent averages.
The181 row descriptors and aggregate definitions say how to combine
these coefficient references, signs and constants. Expanding repeated
giant Fraction rows is unnecessary and is not performed. All binary
offsets, counts, source/artifact hashes and finite scalar data are saved.

Limits of the result
---------------------
There is no automatic ordering between these new rational sextic and
old quartic enclosures: the fixed anchors and error payments differ.
Preserving the old model is essential. Only a verified violation at a
previous exact feasible point, or an exact infeasibility certificate,
could establish an actual improvement in the relaxation for this cell.
Neither test is run here. The earlier indirect SCS result was inaccurate
and is not promoted to an exact point for this packet.

The old model's separate analytic obligations also remain visible; new
normalized-W envelopes do not retroactively prove the old literals. The
packet changes analytic information while retaining the same Fourier
profile, ledger, separation and target. It is not by itself a proof of
the target floor, a complete global cover, an unconditional zero record,
or a website-submittable formal source.
