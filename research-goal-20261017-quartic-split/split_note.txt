1. One rational direction and an exact exhaustive disjunction
-------------------------------------------------------------
Let m=E[y] and C=E[yy^T]-m*m^T at the exact parent relaxation point.
One floating eigendecomposition of this exact 9x9 rational covariance
selected a maximal-eigenvalue direction as a discovery heuristic.  Scale
that vector so its maximum absolute coordinate is 16, round once to
integers, make the first nonzero coordinate positive, and L1 normalize.
There is no direction sweep, alternative quantization, or repeated trial.
The resulting exact direction is

 alpha=(16,16,4,3,2,-7,-15,7,5)/75,
 phi(y)=alpha.y,
 t=alpha.m=-1326025179689503439/37500000000000000000
           approximately -.035360671458386755.

The floating eigensolver is not a mathematical premise.  Only the final
integer alpha and rational t are used by the model and proofs.  Exact
arithmetic verifies sum_i |alpha_i|=1 and -1<t<1.  On the parent cube
|y_i|<=1, the triangle inequality gives -1<=phi<=1.

Split into sigma=-1 and sigma=+1, where sigma*(phi-t)>=0.  Every actual
parent point belongs to at least one side, because phi<=t or phi>=t by
the total ordering of the reals.  Overlap at equality causes no problem.
This is a general halfspace disjunction; neither side is asserted to be
an existing DBM tree node or an axis-aligned child box.

2. Three necessary degree-four localizers per side
---------------------------------------------------
For sigma in {-1,1}, define the positive rational length l=1-sigma*t,

 A=sigma*(phi-t)/l,  B=(1-sigma*phi)/l.

On that side of the parent cube, A>=0, B>=0 and A+B=1.  Therefore A, B,
and 4*A*B are nonnegative.  Their products with the square of any affine
function in y are nonnegative as well.  The corresponding three 10x10
M1 localizing PSD matrices use only the existing shared degree<=4 moments.
There is no new derivative graph variable or scalar sample.

All original 5795 rows and all original 47 PSD blocks are retained.
Each branch has 714 nonconstant gap moments, the same one energy slack,
and 50 PSD blocks of sizes 55 followed by forty-nine 10s.  There are 10030
total conic rows.  The original sublevel localizer is still unsoftened.
Positive rational row/block scales change no mathematical constraint.

All Taylor bounds, dynamic errors, old energy lower L2 and common anchor
are those of the whole parent cell.  Their validity only requires that
the actual scalar arguments lie in the parent intervals.  The old
common anchor need not lie in a halfspace child; it remains a legal
Taylor anchor with the entire original parent segment as the domain.
The artificial halfspace does not change the actual free-KKT hypothesis:
an original interior minimizer still obeys the original nine gradient
equations even if it lies on this artificial splitting plane.

3. Strict rational separation of the old fake covariance, both sides
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For the old parent exact moment point, E[phi]=t.  Consequently the first
added block N=E[A*(1,y)*(1,y)^T] has N00=0.  Its first-row remainder is

 N0,(j+1)=r_j=sigma*(C*alpha)_j/(1-sigma*t).

The exact replay verifies some r_j is nonzero on each side.  Thus the
principal 2x2 minor on indices 0,j+1 has determinant -r_j^2<0.  An even
shorter explicit negative vector uses

 v_(j+1)=1, v_0=-(N_(j+1,j+1)+1)/(2*r_j), all other v_i=0.

Then v^T N v=-1 exactly.  The two negative Fraction vectors and all their
entries are stored in split-model.json.  These are exact witnesses of
strict failure of each child model at the old moment vector, not floating
eigenvalue tests.  A mean point on the splitting plane therefore does
not imply that its nonzero fake covariance is allowed on either side.

This separation is a mathematical information gain over the unsplit
model.  It does not imply that the new models are infeasible: other
moment vectors can satisfy either side.  The convex hull of two branches
can also retain mixtures whose mean lies on the splitting plane.

4. Independent static replay
-----------------------------
split_replay.py imports no producer, numpy, scipy or SCS.  It verifies
the frozen parent manifest, reconstructs alpha/t and A/B/4AB, checks all
six new localizers and all 330 affine entries, and verifies both strict
negative quadratic values -1 using Fraction arithmetic.  It passed in
about .318 seconds.  Its default is read-only and can be invoked again:

 python -S -B -X utf8 research-goal-20261017-quartic-split/split_replay.py

The optional --write-receipt creates split-replay.json only when absent;
it never overwrites a receipt.  The parent static model and strict
feasible-point replays remain separate frozen obligations.
