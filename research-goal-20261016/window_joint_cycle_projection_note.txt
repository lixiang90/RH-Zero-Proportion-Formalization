Joint signed-cycle projection: one strict local band, eight residual tests
2026-10-17

Scope
-----
New standalone note/script/JSON only. No optimizer, Lean, new cover job,
or frozen artifact is modified. This is an actual-kernel cycle route;
actual cosine factors, B/A and C/A are all retained. No global floor,
complete localization, coverage result, or new zero proportion is claimed.
The explicit five-point obstruction below applies ONLY to the selected
32-star-cycle subsystem. It does not refute the complete cycle route.

1. Exact independent cycles and joint energy inequality
-------------------------------------------------------
Use the actual 41 positive ledger edges, a_e>0, and W=diag(a_e).
For each positive span x, put
  A(x)=cos(theta)*pi*x/[Z*(pi^2*x^2-1/2)]>0,
  B(x)/A(x)=-h/x, h=Z/[2*pi*cos(theta)],
  beta(x)=[B(x)*cos(pi*x)+C(x)]/A(x),
  k(x)/A(x)=sin(pi*x)+beta(x).
Here C is the all-24-mode perturbation from the audited exact kernel
decomposition, not a frozen independent coefficient or omitted bias.

Vertex 1 has a positive ledger edge to every other vertex. Use its nine
edges as a star tree, with t_i=|p_i-p_1| and sigma_i=sign(i-1). Each of
the other 32 edges e=(i,j), i<j, gives one independent cycle. Write
  c_i=cos(pi*t_i), k_i=k(t_i), A_i=A(t_i), beta_i=beta(t_i).
The sine difference identity gives exactly
  k_e/A_e-sigma_j*c_i*k_j/A_j+sigma_i*c_j*k_i/A_i
     = D_e=beta_e-sigma_j*c_i*beta_j+sigma_i*c_j*beta_i.    (1)
Let Cmat be this 32-by-41 coefficient matrix, so Cmat*k=D. The chord
columns form the positive diagonal diag(1/A_e); therefore these 32 rows
are independent for every actual configuration, regardless of cosine zeros.
M=Cmat*W^(-1)*Cmat^T is positive definite. Weighted least squares gives
  E=sum_e a_e*k_e^2 >= D^T*M^(-1)*D.                    (2)
The minimizer among vectors satisfying only these linear cycle constraints
is khat=W^(-1)*Cmat^T*M^(-1)*D. This automatically allocates every edge's
energy once, with no sum of overlapping triangle bounds.

A more convenient exact uniform certificate uses ANY fixed real dual y:
  E >= 2*y^T*D-y^T*M*y
     = 2*y^T*D-sum_e [(Cmat^T*y)_e]^2/a_e.              (3)
Indeed subtracting the right side leaves
  sum_e a_e*[k_e-(Cmat^T*y)_e/a_e]^2 >=0.
No numerical inverse, PSD eigenvalue claim, or rank assumption is needed
to validate a fixed y in (3). The script obtains a y by a floating 32-by-32
solve at one specified point and rounds each coefficient to denominator
2^30. All subsequent uniform certificate evaluations use rational outward
intervals and those exact rational y values.

2. Uniform interval semantics and a strict local band
---------------------------------------------------
Every scalar span interval is partitioned on the existing 1/32 SC grid.
The existing 24th-order Taylor provider gives a uniform C/A interval on
each piece; no sample maximum is used. The packets are joined by hull.
The monotonic exact A formula bounds 1/A, and endpoint plus critical-point
trigonometric intervals bound cos(pi*x). Beta is evaluated as
  beta= -h*cos(pi*x)/x + C/A.
Signed y*D and every component of Cmat^T*y are then accumulated before
the weighted squares in (3). The receipt records every 41-edge hull,
piece count and piece endpoint hash, and the exact rational resulting bound.
Analytic soundness inherits the explicit C Taylor remainder and frozen
table semantics in window_phase_coefficients.py; this is not a Lean proof.

Consider the low-pressure integer configuration g=(1,...,1). Define a
coherent span band by |(p_j-p_i)-(j-i)|<=r for every one of the 45 pairs.
For r<=1/32 its adjacent gaps exceed 123/128. One fixed rational dual from
the centre gives the following UNIFORM interval-evidence lower bounds:

  r       kernel energy E lower       pressure+E lower
  1/128   0.006283013421879783         0.010670732850004784
  1/64    0.005772501962567189         0.010125672418817190
  1/32    0.004683209341791693         0.008967281854291693

All decimal entries summarize exact rational lower endpoints in the JSON.
The script checks literally, over the rationals, for r=1/32:
  E_lower > 4683/10^6,
  F_lower > 8967/10^6 > 221/25000 = 0.00884.
The pressure lower bound in this band is exactly
  (31/32)*sum_r b_r = 0.0042840725125.
Thus multiple signed cycles can pay much more than a single triangle's
1e-5 local energy. This is one specified local band and has no global
coverage consequence or zero-proportion increment.

At the centre, floating diagnostics give E=0.00860120690487 and the joint
32-cycle projection bound 0.00677198914686; the best single-cycle bound
there is only 0.000160273515993. For g=(9/4,1,...,1), which defeated the
earlier unsigned phase-energy strategy, this joint bound is 0.006016326925
at the point. Those centre values are floating diagnostics, not uniform
certificates, and the uniform band table is the paid statement.

3. Eight frozen residual cells: uniform payment fails for this fixed dual
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The script reads precisely the eight paths in cover-lifted-graph-energy8.json
and the corresponding 'before' states in cover-curvature-parent64.input.json.gz.
Each chosen prefix midpoint is checked over the rationals to lie in its
entire 45-pair DBM cell. No cell or selection is optimized or replaced.

In selection order, the pointwise floating 32-cycle kernel-energy lower
bounds are
  .00257134, .00289451, .00349093, .00420892,
  .00340890, .00357704, .00303138, .00312836.
The largest single-cycle bounds are .000087 to .000938. The 32-by-32
Gram condition numbers at these points are about 85 to 253; they are
floating diagnostics, not uniform condition certificates.

Using each centre's same fixed rational dual over its FULL residual cell
gives negative lower endpoints:
  -.0249405, -.1847174, -.0362027, -.1820959,
  -.0742551, -.2200665, -.1565909, -.0227567.
These are valid but unhelpful interval payments. They establish only that
this particular uniform fixed-dual evaluation does not exclude those cells;
they do not prove that no better dual or correlated bound can work.

There is also a strict point obstruction for the selected 32-cycle
subsystem. Choose the nine star components of the floating least-squares
minimizer, round them to rational denominator 2^30, and define each chord
component by the exact formula solving (1):
  khat_e=A_e*[D_e+sigma_j*c_i*khat_j/A_j
                   -sigma_i*c_j*khat_i/A_i].
Then Cmat*khat=D holds exactly by construction. Rational intervals on an
outward one-SC-unit enclosure of each exact midpoint span bound the cost
sum a_e*khat_e^2 from ABOVE. This witnesses a feasible energy for that
projection relaxation. The vectors khat are NOT the actual kernel vectors.

For selection indices 0,1,4,6,7 the certified upper bounds on P+projection
cost are respectively approximately
  .008286146443618, .008590570944093, .008627502795415,
  .008669571232769, .008764836834060,
all strictly below .0088 by exact rational comparisons in the receipt.
No uniform bound obtained from ONLY these 32 cycle equations and energy
minimization can exclude those five whole cells, because each contains
such a feasible relaxed point. These are not actual F<.0088 points and
need not satisfy actual minimizer KKT. Additional cycle constraints can
invalidate them; the next diagnostic shows this matters.

4. Complete triangle diagnostic: explicitly not a route obstruction
------------------------------------------------------------------
There are 90 positive-edge triangles. For each ordered i<j<l, the exact
addition row is
  cos(pi*x_jl)*k_ij/A_ij + cos(pi*x_ij)*k_jl/A_jl-k_il/A_il
    = cos(pi*x_jl)*beta_ij+cos(pi*x_ij)*beta_jl-beta_il.
The script checks these identities numerically at the same ten points
and applies an SVD of the 90-by-41 whitened matrix. No optimization runs.
At all eight residual midpoints the numerical rank is 40 (relative
singular cutoff 1e-10); their full-triangle projection lower bounds on E
are approximately
  .00519265, .00551806, .00483971, .00485651,
  .00545859, .00473426, .00553369, .00498830.
Adding the actual midpoint pressures puts all eight above .0088 at those
points. This is floating pointwise evidence only, with no whole-cell
claim, rank proof, rationalized full-triangle dual, or exclusion certificate.
The actual sine vector A_e*sin(pi*x_e) lies in the homogeneous nullspace.
Numerical rank 40 suggests that it may be the only generic null direction,
but that is an inference, not a theorem established here. At both special
low-pressure test points the numerical rank drops to 32. In particular,
integer-phase degeneration cannot be ignored or paid by a generic inverse.

The five strict witnesses above disprove sufficiency ONLY for the chosen
32-star-cycle subsystem. They say nothing adverse about a correctly paid
complete-cycle method retaining its extra independent constraints.
