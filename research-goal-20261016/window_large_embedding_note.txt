Arbitrary larger-block reuse of the two old nine-point certificates
2026-10-17

One bounded LP tested all 12,870 translation classes of nine-point subsets
of seventeen ordered vertices. This includes the ten-point delete-one
construction and many sparse, nonconsecutive embeddings. The scalar kernel
is unchanged; its old unconditional floor supplies every embedded floor.
Only the pair/pressure ledger changes. No nine-dimensional numerical cover
is needed for this construction class.

The LP did not produce the requested improvement. An exact nonnegative
rank dual was rounded and repaired. A standard-library-only implementation
then reparsed both the locally proved and the current website nine-point
ledgers, verified that their thirteen scalar-profile coefficients agree,
and checked all 25,740 finite columns. A single uniform rational repair
makes the same dual valid for both ledgers and their nonnegative mixtures.
No second optimizer was used.

There is an additional finite-to-infinite step. Translate any embedding
so its first vertex is zero. If its last vertex is at most sixteen, its
column was checked. Otherwise its eight positive integer index gaps have
at least nine extra units. Its total pressure satisfies

  B_embedding >= sum(b_old) + 9*min(b_old) > K*c_old,

where K is the website score plus 1/100000. Its target profit is therefore
negative and every nonnegative dual pays it automatically. Thus increasing
the ambient block size cannot escape this particular obstruction.

For either old certificate family, the exact finite column inequalities
are

  K*c_old - B_embedding <= sum_r y_r*a_embedding_rank_r,

with y_r=0 beyond rank sixteen. For any finite nonnegative combination of
these embedded inequalities and rank masses <=2, summation gives

  K*C - B_total <= 2*sum(y) < K-H.

Consequently (H-B_total)/(1-C)<K in the valid counting regime C<1.
The strict shortfall is about 9.8278e-6 in this linear objective. The
exact fraction and the two outside-span margins are in the replay receipt.
The fixed H is the older profile enclosure used in the preceding ledger
experiment; it is slightly more optimistic than the already proved local
HW_ge, not a fresh improvement or assertion about every possible H bound.

This rules out obtaining the requested gain solely by nonnegative reuse
of these two frozen old nine-point certificates in arbitrarily large
ordered blocks, with this H and the stated rank capacities. It does not
rule out a new kernel, new local analytic information, signed combinations
with separately justified residuals, or the outstanding W10 energy floor.
It also does not rule out better finite-dimensional certificates for the
same old kernel. No new unconditional zero proportion follows.

solver or producer imports. Initial receipt generation is single-use and

inputs, dual, repair, all-column minimum slack and strict target gap.
The file manifest separately binds all source and receipt hashes.

The rest of the project still lacks a complete global root cover, final
unconditional website theorem, independent replay of all new roots, trusted
statement comparison and the final one-file source-size check.
