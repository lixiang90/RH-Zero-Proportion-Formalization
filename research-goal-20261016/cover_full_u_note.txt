Full cross-derivative moment model: strict new information, first bounded test
2026-10-17

This package preserves the first full 51x51 joint-PSD experiment unchanged.
It adds genuine derivative-derivative cross constraints to the old 464
variables.  An independent polynomial replay checks every added rational
row and Gram entry.  The one numerical solve did not converge; it provides
neither a feasible-point certificate nor an exclusion certificate.

1. Variables and normalization
------------------------------
The cell is the first original remaining case,
r001000001000100110011001.  All nine gap coordinates are strictly above
the fixed separation, so a true minimum in this cell has nine free KKT
equations H_r(u)=b_r+sum_(e contains r)a_e*u_e=0.

Retain the old 464 variables: physical gap means g[9], derivative means
u[41], normalized position products yy[45], and mixed products yu[369].
Add all 41*42/2=861 derivative product variables.  The total is 1325.

For conditioning, the new variables are W_et=<v_e*v_t>, where the existing
exact derivative graph box is [l_e,h_e], mu_e=(l_e+h_e)/2,
r_e=(h_e-l_e)/2>0, and v_e=(u_e-mu_e)/r_e.  This is an invertible affine
change of moment coordinates.  The physical cross derivatives are retained:

  U_et=<u_e*u_t>
      = r_e*r_t*W_et + mu_e*<u_t> + mu_t*<u_e> - mu_e*mu_t.

The full 51x51 joint matrix is the moment matrix of
(1,y_0,...,y_8,v_0,...,v_40), where the old normalized y coordinates are
unchanged.  The model records its 1326 rational affine lower entries.
No Gram-minimal completion is substituted for U in the solver model.

2. Constraints and actual-point validity
-----------------------------------------
All original 43,306 rows, including the original midpoint and closed
Bregman energy packets, and every old finite box are retained exactly.
The following 4264 rows are added:

  41 old affine-slab residual-square upper bounds;
  41 dynamic-sector residual-square upper bounds;
  3444 derivative-box product RLT inequalities;
  738 inequality rows encoding 369 u-multiplied free-KKT equalities.

For a residual beta_e.x, the square upper rows are

  U_ee-2*beta_e^T*z_e+beta_e^T*X*beta_e <= B_e.

For an old slab B_e=epsilon_e^2; for the dynamic sector it is the exact
dynamic expression gamma_e*<d_e^2>+1025*epsilon0_e^2.  Thus none of the
old dynamic second moments is replaced by a maximum radius constant.

The joint PSD implies the Gram block of (x,u_e-beta_e.x) is PSD.  Increasing
its lower-right entry from its actual residual second moment to B_e proves
the corresponding old independent 11x11 cone.  Consequently joint PSD plus
these 82 scalar rows implies all 82 former independent cones; replacing
their numerical representation by this one does not weaken their content.

The 3444 RLT rows are all four nonnegative products

  <(1+s*v_e)*(1+t*v_f)> >= 0,  s,t in {-1,1}, e<=f.

Their diagonal cases include W_ee<=1 and lower tangent inequalities.
The new boxes are W_ee in [0,1], off-diagonal W_ef in [-1,1].  These follow
at every actual derivative-box point and under a nonnegative average.

For every r=0,...,8 and t=0,...,40, retain

  <u_t*H_r(u)> = b_r*<u_t> + sum_(e contains r)a_e*U_te = 0.

Every equality is valid at an actual free-KKT point; no actual-box point
is asserted to satisfy KKT automatically.  The independent replay expands
these products directly, not through a projected completion.  New row
normalizations are explicit positive rational scalars and do not alter
their semantics.  Only the two original energy rows receive the unchanged
numerical energy-slack variable in the SCS discovery program.

3. Why the old independent-cone compression does not apply
---------------------------------------------------------
With only full joint PSD and upper bounds on individual residual squares,
projecting every derivative Gram vector onto the span of x preserves the
old moments, reduces every residual norm, and can satisfy the multiplied
KKT equations.  This explains the earlier compression equivalence.  It
also applies when the upper bound B_e depends affinely on old moments.

Derivative-product RLT inequalities have lower as well as upper cross
moment requirements.  They are not preserved by decreasing U in PSD order.
The new model explicitly retains those requirements.  A strict rational
toy proves the difference even with y-u box RLT already present:

  X=diag(1,1/4), <y>=0,
  z1=(<u1>,<y*u1>)=(1/2,2/5),
  z2=(<u2>,<y*u2>)=(1/2,-2/5),
  y,u1,u2 boxes [-1,1], each individual residual bound <u_i^2><=1.

Each independent cone [[X,z_i],[z_i^T,1]] has Schur complement 11/100>0.
Every y-u box RLT row has slack at least 1/10.  The position square 1/4
also satisfies its PSD and box moment conditions.  The KKT toy
H=u1+u2-1 has both <H>=0 and <y*H>=0.

Full joint PSD applied to u1-u2-(16/5)*y forces

  <(u1-u2)^2> >= 64/25.

But derivative-box RLT gives U12>=<u1>+<u2>-1=0, and the diagonal bounds
give U11,U22<=1.  Hence <(u1-u2)^2><=2, a contradiction with exact margin
14/25.  The Gram-minimal U is [[89/100,-39/100],[-39/100,89/100]].  It
satisfies the multiplied KKT equalities but violates the new lower cross
bound U12>=0.  Thus uKKT alone is not the new information; preserving UU
cross-RLT is essential.  Duplicating looser independent upper cones cannot
repair this missing cross constraint.  This is an algebraic nonimplication
example, not an actual-kernel or actual-minimizer counterexample.

On the real cell's old exact 464 point, the Gram-minimal normalized W
violates 15 UU-RLT rows.  The static two-coordinate PSD-room necessary
tests, however, find no strict obstruction to a different extension under
the old 41 slab bounds.  No U completion is claimed from this diagnostic.
The same old point is already excluded by seven dynamic-sector cones, so
it cannot itself establish infeasibility or feasibility of the new model.

4. First full51 numerical test and its limit
------------------------------------------
cover_full_u_joint_cone.py makes one SCS 3.3.1 call with 1325 scalar moments
and the original slack.  The model has 50,221 linear rows after boxes and
one packed 51x51 PSD block.  All original and new rational rows and the
affine Gram map are bound by hashes.  A factorized map test is performed
before solving.  Limits are 45 seconds and 10,000 iterations.

The one call used 10,000 iterations and about 29.44 seconds.  SCS returned
"solved (inaccurate - reached max_iters)" with:

  raw energy slack        -7.49e-8,
  worst linear slack     -7.49e-5,
  worst added-row slack  -3.07e-5,
  minimum joint eigenvalue about -.009816.

These are material constraint violations, so the output is not a feasible
point.  It also provides no positive exclusion margin.  No exact dual,
Farkas extraction, LP polish, or repeated full51 solve was attempted.
The first result remains frozen as a numerical failure.

The nine free KKT equations force at least nine null directions in the
joint Gram matrix: mean KKT, old y*KKT, and the new u*KKT together give
M*A=0.  Therefore strict joint-PSD feasibility is impossible in this
representation, even when a valid model point exists.  This is a concrete
reason to examine an exact face reduction as a separate computational
representation; it is not proof that this alone caused nonconvergence.
Any later reduction must preserve the same mathematical constraints and
have its own source, output, proof of equivalence, and numerical budget.

5. Independent audit and scope
-------------------------------
cover_full_u_replay.py imports only the standard library.  It reconstructs
all 4264 rows by multiplying polynomials in actual y/u variables and then
substituting the declared moments, and independently reconstructs all
1326 joint Gram entries.  It checks the strict toy and the exact 14/25 gap.
The old scalar/geometry/energy packets remain previously frozen analytic
and finite inputs; this task makes no new actual-kernel evaluation or
Lean invocation.  Prior packages 5995..., 26c0..., 240f..., and 7ae320...
are unchanged.  No whole cell or global cover is proved and no new zero
proportion follows from this package.
