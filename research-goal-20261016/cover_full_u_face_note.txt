Exact free-KKT face reduction of the full derivative moment model

This package changes only the computational representation of the frozen
full-U model.  It adds no mathematical inequality, removes no box or other

1. KKT nullspace and exact factorization
----------------------------------------
The full Gram feature vector is z=(1,y[9],v[41]), dimension 51, with
v_e=(u_e-mu_e)/sigma_e and sigma_e>0 from the exact derivative box.
Let A have nine columns encoding the free KKT linear forms

  H_r = C_r*z0 + sum_(e contains r) a_e*sigma_e*v_e,
  C_r = b_r+sum_(e contains r)a_e*mu_e.

The old mean and y-multiplied KKT equalities, together with the newly
retained u-multiplied KKT equalities, imply M*A=0 for the full symmetric
moment matrix M.  The normalized v versions follow by subtracting mu_e
times the mean equation and dividing by sigma_e.  The independent replay
checks every one of these 51*9=459 null equations as an exact linear
combination of the actually retained rows, rather than merely assuming
that KKT products were present.

For each gap r, the adjacent edge e_r=(r,r+1) occurs only in equation r.
Its coefficient a_e_r*sigma_e_r is strictly positive.  Thus the nine
adjacent derivatives are exact nonzero diagonal pivots.  Solve their
features in terms of z0 and the 32 nonadjacent derivative features:

  v_e_r = -C_r/(a_e_r*sigma_e_r)
          -sum_(nonadjacent e contains r)
             (a_e*sigma_e)/(a_e_r*sigma_e_r)*v_e.

Keep q=(1,y[9],v_nonadjacent[32]), dimension 42.  Let T be the resulting
51x42 rational feature map, and S the selector of the 42 kept rows.  The
following identities are checked exactly:

  S*T=I42, A^T*T=0, I51-T*S=J*A^T,

where J has only the nine adjacent pivot rows, with entries equal to the
reciprocals of those positive pivots.  In particular T has full column
rank.  For every symmetric M satisfying M*A=0, symmetry and the last
identity give

  M=T*(S*M*S^T)*T^T.

Hence full51 PSD is equivalent to PSD of the kept principal block
Q=S*M*S^T, size 42.  Conversely M=T*Q*T^T reconstructs all KKT-null
moments.  This statement is about the forced KKT face, not about
projecting derivatives onto the ten-dimensional position Gram span.

2. All moment variables and constraints are mapped
--------------------------------------------------
The 42 Gram features have 42*43/2-1=902 nonconstant moments.  The constant
entry is Q00=1.  All 1325 original moments are affine expressions in these
902 coordinates.  The eliminated coordinates are nine derivative means,
81 position/derivative products, and 333 derivative/derivative products,
423 in total.  The remaining 528 nonadjacent derivative cross moments
are independent coordinates; cross-RLT information is not discarded.

The original physical gaps, derivative means, h=yy, q=yu, and normalized
W=vv are recovered using the exact T map and the unchanged box normalizers.
The producer checks all 1326 original joint Gram entries after substitution
against T*Q*T^T.  The independent replay reconstructs the map separately
and also checks all 902 kept-entry inverse identities.  This proves that
the change of variables has the stated left inverse.

Every one of the original 50,221 linear rows, including all 1325 original
boxes and the same slack condition, is pulled back exactly.  The full
original row digest matches the frozen full51 solver's row digest before
any reduction.  The energy slack remains in units of .001 and is present
only on the same two original energy rows.  The objective is unchanged.

The reduction removes exactly 1080 tautologies (the KKT equations and
their implied products) and 2794 positive-proportional duplicates.  Every
removal is recorded in an original-row map.  A retained inequality is
normalized by its positive rational maximum coefficient/bound magnitude.
The remaining model has 46,347 distinct rows.  The independent replay
checks every original pullback against the retained row and its exact
positive scale, or verifies that it is a nonnegative constant tautology.
It therefore also checks every original box mapping.  No finite-width,
curvature, derivative, RLT, dynamic-sector, or energy condition was weakened.

The large model is a strict-JSON gzip file with deterministic compression.
The build receipt records compressed and decompressed hashes.  All scalar
parameters and coefficients are exact Fraction strings or integers.

3. One separately authorized reduced42 numerical test
-----------------------------------------------------
After the exact static identities passed, one SCS 3.3.1 solve used 902
moment variables plus the original slack, 46,347 linear rows, and one
42x42 PSD cone.  Limits were 45 seconds and 10,000 iterations.  This was
not a repeated full51 run: it eliminates the nine forced PSD null
directions, 423 dependent moments, and the stated redundant rows exactly.

The one call took about 34.92 seconds and reached 10,000 iterations with
status "solved (inaccurate - reached max_iters)":

  raw energy slack             -3.32e-10,
  worst normalized row slack   -3.32e-7,
  minimum reduced eigenvalue   -8.04e-9.

The numerical residuals are much smaller than for the frozen full51
representation, but remain nonzero.  There is no positive exclusion
margin and no exact feasible point.  No second reduced solve, parameter
change, dual extraction, LP polish, or additional case was attempted.
The output is discovery data only.  Unused nonfinite SCS diagnostic fields
are serialized as strings, so the receipt is valid strict JSON.

4. Independent replay and scope
--------------------------------
cover_full_u_face_replay.py imports only the standard library and the
previously audited exact-row algebra interface.  It verifies all 459
KKT-null linear combinations, every T/J/S identity, all moment maps,
1326 Gram identities, 902 inverse checks, and all 50,221 row/box pullbacks.
It confirms 46,347 distinct retained rows, 1080 tautologies and 2794 positive
duplicates.  It imports no face-reduction producer or numerical package.

This is an exact computational representation improvement of the same
full-U mathematical model.  The earlier full-U cross-RLT model is the
mathematical strengthening; this face reduction is not a further one.
No actual-kernel floor, whole-cell exclusion, global cover or new zero
proportion is established here.  All earlier frozen packages are unchanged.
