Mathematical assumptions
------------------------
Let f=w24,d24, u_e=f'(s_e), v_e=f''(s_e), and c_e be the incidence vector
of the consecutive gap coordinates i,...,j-1 for edge e=(i,j). The exact
weights a_e are positive. At a global orthant minimizer with every gap
strictly above sep, every real direction d has

  d^T H d = sum_e a_e v_e (c_e^T d)^2 >= 0,
  H = sum_e a_e v_e c_e c_e^T.

WindowSecondVariation.curvature_nonneg_of_relevant proves this actual
second-variation condition. The first nine lower cell bounds are strictly
above the original separation constraint, so no artificial cell boundary
is treated as a feasible-domain boundary. The linear pressure has zero
Hessian. WindowSecondVariation.w2_eq_actual identifies the scalar v with
the actual second derivative. No extra positional exclusion is assumed.

Exact graph facets
------------------
The existing newjet u-grid and SecondOracle v-grid use the same 1/64
intervals. For an intersected grid box write

  s in [a,b]/SC, u in [L,U]/DS, v in [J,K]/DS,
  SC=32768, DS=10^15.

The v-grid is independently recomputed from the frozen rational interval
Taylor coefficients of u: interval Horner evaluation of its derivative
on the same grid, enlarged by (44/7)^24*(1/2)^22/22!. The replay checks
658 distinct v-grid intervals, all 1453 edge/grid rectangles, and their
identity with the saved boxes. Existing newjet coefficients and their
analytic derivative-remainder premises are inherited unchanged.

A floating 3D ConvexHull of the eight physical-coordinate box corners
only suggests outward normals. Each normal is rounded to integer n with
maximum proposed scale 10^6 and divided by its positive gcd. Six signed
coordinate normals are included. For every such integer n=(ns,nu,nv),
the exact intercept numerator is

 C = max_boxes { ns*X*DS + SC*(nu*Y + nv*Z) },
 X=b if ns>=0 else a; Y=U if nu>=0 else L; Z=K if nv>=0 else J.

The valid affine constraint is

  ns*s + nu*u + nv*v <= C/(SC*DS).

The maximum is recomputed over every rectangle, not just hull vertices,
after rounding the normal. The replay independently repeats the maximum
over all eight corners of every rectangle. No floating hull intercept is
trusted. There are 6075 distinct edge facets, of which 4047 have both
nu != 0 and nv != 0. No RLT products involving the new v are introduced.

Rational extension of the frozen vector
--------------------------------------
Fix s and u from the frozen exact 464-vector. Each new facet becomes an
affine upper/lower bound on its one v_e (or a satisfied v-free row).
Every edge has a nonempty rational interval [lo_e,up_e]. Choose v_e=up_e.
Every saved facet is then checked exactly. Because a_e>0 and c_e c_e^T
is PSD, H is monotone in each v_e in the Loewner order. Thus this is also
the largest Hessian available inside these per-edge facet intervals for
the fixed old (s,u). This choice is deterministic, not an optimization.

The resulting physical H has an exact rational unit-lower LDL^T
factorization with nine strictly positive pivots. Decimal displays only:

The replay stores the exact rational v vector, L, and positive diagonal;

strictly positive at this outer-relaxation extension. The earlier trial
