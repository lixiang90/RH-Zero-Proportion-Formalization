Affine kernel-floor transfer: a static feasibility design and a rigorous obstruction
2026-10-17

Scope
-----
This note examines a way to reuse an already unconditional nine-point local
floor, rather than prove another nine-dimensional minimizer cover.  It gives
the precise semi-infinite LP interface and proves that its natural unscaled,
edgewise-affine version is too weak for the frozen new 24-mode window.  This is
a limitation of a certificate class, not a counterexample to the desired
actual local floor, and not an upper bound on any actual zeta-zero density.
No optimizer, Lean, or global cover was invoked for this audit.

The earlier exact residual-PSD relaxation obstruction is a separate sealed
package, manifest SHA256
5995d2ba510e40e31edbd17d3337a4f39c89acc62e209c016b0d584464714c6b.
Nothing in that package or the preceding 26c0d025... package was modified.

2. Exact transfer interface
---------------------------
For a new edge e=(i,j), put v_e[r]=1 if i<=r<j, zero otherwise, and
x_e=v_e dot g.  Let a_e be its frozen new weight.  There are ten ordered
nine-point subconfigurations of ten points: delete one vertex d, d=0,...,9.
For such a deletion let p_d be the old pressure vector expressed in the nine
new gaps, and let A_(d,e) be the old weight transported to the new edge e.
The actual old floor gives

  p_d dot g + sum_e A_(d,e) w_old(x_e) >= c_old.

Select nonnegative multipliers lambda_d.  Write
alpha=sum_d lambda_d and A_e=sum_d lambda_d A_(d,e).  An affine edgewise
transfer certificate consists of rational q_e, d_e, and rho_r such that

  a_e w_new(x) + q_e*x >= A_e w_old(x) + d_e
       for every x >= (j-i)*s,                              (T1)
  b_new - sum_d lambda_d p_d - sum_e q_e v_e = rho,          (T2)
  lambda_d >= 0, rho_r >= 0.                               (T3)

Then exact summation, followed by the old floor and g_r>=s, proves

  F_new(g) >= alpha*c_old + sum_e d_e + s*sum_r rho_r.      (T4)

Thus the natural search is a semi-infinite LP: maximize (T4), with one
univariate nonnegativity family (T1) per new edge.  A proposed rational
solution could be audited through interval/envelope univariate proofs and
a tail bound.  No nine-dimensional cover is needed if this LP succeeds.

The data also show that omitting q_e and simply requiring coefficientwise
domination cannot transfer any positive copy of any one deletion: every
one of the ten deletions transports at least one strictly positive old
weight onto a zero new weight.  All ten lists of these holes are recorded
in window-kernel-floor-transfer-design.json.  This observation alone would
not exclude a compensated certificate (T1)-(T3); the next argument does.

3. Root/pressure upper bound on the whole certificate class
----------------------------------------------------------
Lemma.  Suppose w_new,w_old are nonnegative continuous functions tending
to zero at positive infinity.  Suppose for each new edge rank r=1,...,9
there is a zero z_r of w_new in [r*s,C*r], where C>=s.  Every transfer
certificate (T1)-(T3) obeys

  alpha*c_old + sum_e d_e + s*sum_r rho_r
    <= alpha*c_old + C*(B_new-alpha*B_old).                 (U)

Proof.  In (T1) let x tend to positive infinity.  If q_e<0 the left side
minus the old kernel term tends to negative infinity, contradicting the
finite d_e.  Hence q_e>=0.  At x=z_(j-i), new energy vanishes and the old
term is nonnegative; thus

  d_e <= q_e*z_(j-i) - A_e*w_old(z_(j-i))
      <= C*(j-i)*q_e.

Sum (T2) over the nine coordinates.  Since sum_r v_e[r]=j-i,

  sum_e (j-i)*q_e = B_new - sum_d lambda_d*sum_r p_d[r]
                              - sum_r rho_r.

Deleting an interior point merges two gaps: the old pressure on that old
gap is charged to two new gaps.  Consequently sum_r p_d[r]>=B_old, with
equality for either endpoint deletion.  It follows that sum_e d_e plus
s*sum rho is at most

  C*(B_new-alpha*B_old) - (C-s)*sum rho
    <= C*(B_new-alpha*B_old).

This proves (U).  It permits arbitrary real/rational d_e and arbitrary
univariate proofs of (T1).  It does not rely on a sampled LP optimum.

Every deletion has p_d[4]=b_old[3]=b_old[4]: central new gap 4 belongs to
old gap 3 if the deleted vertex is on its left, or old gap 4 otherwise.
Since q_e>=0 and rho>=0, coordinate 4 of (T2) implies

  alpha <= b_new[4]/b_old[3].                              (A)

For both old ledgers c_old-C*B_old>0.  Substitution of the maximal alpha
from (A) into (U) is therefore a valid upper bound for every certificate.
Notice that old energies and most coordinate pressure constraints were
dropped to derive it.  Restoring them can only lower the upper bound.

The finite sinc-sum identity gives k_new(x)=O(1/x) as x tends to infinity,
and likewise for k_old.  Their squares satisfy the required decay and
nonnegativity.  A rational interval audit supplies the zero-existence
inputs with C=11/10.  For each rank r it evaluates k_new at

  left_r = max(r*123/128, r-1/10), right_r = r+1/10.

The two endpoint enclosures have strictly opposite signs for all r=1,...,9.
These endpoints lie in [r*s,C*r].  Continuity and IVT therefore give the
roots.  The audit uses 18 exact rational endpoint computations, not a root
optimizer.  The interval implementation uses Machin's arctangent identity,
alternating-series atan bounds, a Taylor sin enclosure after range reduction,
and an integer-square-root enclosure.  The chosen integer for range
reduction can be selected by a float because the full reduced rational
interval is subsequently checked; no rounding decision is trusted.
The actual integral/sinc identity and those standard analytic interval
facts are explicit analytic inputs.  They were not newly checked in Lean.

4. Quantitative outcome
-----------------------
With the locally formalized old floor:

  alpha <= 182571/190370 = .959032410568892157...,
  transfer floor <=
    161632154665702160441651/19037000000000000000000000
    = .008490421529952311837... .

Even using the archived website's better old ledger:

  alpha <= 60857/63415 = .959662540408420720...,
  transfer floor <=
    4517641103805188655989325867/531963576320000000000000000000
    = .008492388022234861600... .

Both are strictly below c_required by more than .0002775.  Substituting
these method ceilings in the counting formula with the frozen H_new would
give at most 67.3391448663% and 67.3392784224%, respectively.  These are
ceilings on what this transfer proof can report with the frozen H bound,
not upper bounds on the actual zero proportion or actual F_new minimum.

Mixtures of the two old ledger families cannot improve this ceiling.
Their objective contributions above C*B_new are
lambda_t*(c_t-C*B_t), while the same central budget is
sum_t lambda_t*b_t[3]<=b_new[4].  Hence use the larger ratio
(c_t-C*B_t)/b_t[3]; its maximum is the archived website family.  The mixed
ceiling is therefore the same .008492388022234861600... .

5. Remaining variants and why no optimization was launched
----------------------------------------------------------
This obstruction excludes the unscaled old-kernel comparison w_old(x),
all ten delete-one subconfigurations, their nonnegative combinations,
arbitrary affine edge payments, arbitrary constant edge floors, and a
nonnegative unused pressure residue.  Merely improving the univariate
interval tables or selecting a different LP solver cannot bypass (U).

It does not exclude a genuinely different comparison.  For example apply
the old floor to scaled configurations t*D_d(g).  Then the old kernel is
w_old(t*x) and old pressure is t*p_d.  Dropping the old root penalties as
above gives the optimistic bound

  U(t)=C*B_new + (b_new[4]/b_old[3])*(c_old/t-C*B_old).

A necessary condition for this loose upper bound to reach c_required is
t<=.9672086190... locally, or t<=.9674450555... for the website ledger.
These are necessary, not sufficient, and not a discovery of a transfer:
at a new-kernel zero, w_old(t*z_r) is generally positive and imposes the
additional negative payments that (U(t)) deliberately discarded.  Testing
scaled comparisons would require new uniform univariate inequalities; no
such optimization or claim is made in this task.

Comparisons restricted to an already proved compact sublevel domain can
also escape the q_e>=0 tail argument.  They must explicitly use the new
global pressure constraint or upper gap bounds, and pay the resulting
signed linear residues.  Coupled multi-span inequalities or a nonlinear
telescoping potential can use additive geometry absent from (T1).  None
is supplied here.  The current result instead shows a concrete reason why
the most direct scalar kernel-majorization transfer cannot replace the
missing whole-domain proof for this new window.
