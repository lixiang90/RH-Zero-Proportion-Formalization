2. Exactly one new whole-cell dual: collect shared edges before payment
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Only case 0 is tried, at its unchanged prefix centre. Its 90 actual positive
triangle rows satisfy Cmat*k=D and Cmat*u=0. A single centre SVD gives a
minimum-norm dual hint, rounded to rational denominator 2^40. There is no
dual optimization, LP, SDP, repeated rank-cut loop or second dual attempt.
Uniform dual validity holds for ANY rational multipliers; numerical rank
40 is discovery information and is not used as a theorem hypothesis.

Write beta_e=v_e/A_e and collect exactly, over the rationals,
  H_e(g)=A_e*(Cmat(g)^T*y)_e
        =constant_e+sum_f coefficient_ef*cos(pi*x_f).
All constants and repeated cosine coefficients are combined BEFORE any
interval arithmetic. The complete shared-edge identity is
  y.D=sum_e H_e*beta_e,
and the actual energy dual is therefore
  E >= L(g)=sum_e [2*H_e*beta_e-H_e^2/(a_e*A_e^2)].       (1)
This pays each beta only once, after cycle cancellation. The older star
packet had collected the quadratic term by edge but paid its linear y.D
cycle by cycle; the shared linear payment here is new.

Uniform beta, cosine and inverse-A hulls are reused from the already frozen
case-0 scalar packets. The exact span endpoints and all source hashes are
checked against the original parent state and current frozen parameter,
Taylor provider and table sources. No new analytic scalar enclosure is
silently substituted.

There is one further elementary improvement over independent interval
evaluation: if H in [hL,hU], beta in [bL,bU] and rho=1/(a*A^2)<=rhoU,
  2*H*beta-rho*H^2 >=
     min_{h in {hL,hU}, b in {bL,bU}} [2*h*b-rhoU*h^2].
For fixed b the expression is concave in h, and for fixed h it is affine
in b, so the four-corner minimum is a uniform lower bound. All four-corner
comparisons and their sum use Fractions. This retains the shared H within
its linear and quadratic terms instead of charging two independent H boxes.
