An exact feasible point of the current outer relaxation
2026-10-17

This package certifies a limitation of one finite moment relaxation. It
does NOT give an actual Fw<c configuration, refute the proposed floor,
prove a global cover, or improve a zero proportion.

The one tested case is r001000001000100110011001, the first of the six
previously frozen remaining cells. The model retains 43,304 old graph,
DBM, first-order KKT, KKT-times-position, RLT and McCormick rows, together
with the original midpoint energy and the closed affine-h Bregman energy
row. All nine gap coordinates are strictly above the original 123/128
boundary. It includes all 41 eleven-dimensional residual PSD cones, not
only the finite cuts used in the earlier exploratory LP.

Construction
------------
The root's joint SCS numerical point was decimalized once by F(str(float)).
The nine adjacent-edge derivative variables were solved exactly from
  b_r+sum_(i<=r<j) a_ij*u_ij=0.
For each normalized position y_s, the corresponding 81 adjacent mixed
variables were solved from
  b_r*y_s+sum_(i<=r<j) a_ij*q_(s,ij)=0.
Every adjacent weight is positive and its derivative occurs in exactly
one coordinate equation, so these are independent explicit scalar solves.
The normalized y values are recomputed from the same exact rational g,
not rounded independently. No second precision choice, adjustment of g
or the position moments, solver, or additional search was used.

All 43,306 rational inequalities and all 464 finite boxes pass exactly.
All 90 KKT equalities are exact. Each normalized residual matrix has
bottom diagonal 1 and off-diagonal residual divided by its positive
rational epsilon. The certificate provides an exact unit-lower LDL
factorization with strictly positive rational diagonal for all 41 blocks.
There is no SCS sqrt(2) packing or numerical eigenvalue in these checks.

The scalar graph ingredients and complete old rows are reconstructed by
the stdlib replay directly from the frozen graph grid and Taylor tuples.
It then independently rebuilds the exact moment model, checks every row,
and verifies each LDL identity. No NumPy, SciPy, SCS, producer, or floating
linear algebra is imported by that replay.

Numerical displays for context only: the two exact energy-row slacks are
approximately 0.0103372090195 and 0.000302297678602. The smallest normalized
LDL pivot is about 0.0354370. Exact fractions are in the certificate.

Interpretation
--------------
The point's nine position variances are strictly positive, whereas an
actual point evaluation would have all of them zero. It is therefore
explicitly a pseudo-moment point, not an actual gap/derivative assignment.
Neither a representing probability measure nor actual kernel identities
u=w'(span) are asserted by this feasibility certificate.

The result proves that the present full residual-PSD relaxation plus the
closed Bregman energy row cannot exclude this cell. More necessary PSD
cuts from the same 41 complete cones cannot change this conclusion.
Additional constraints, higher-order information, or a different cover
could still be stronger; no limitation on those is proved here.

Under the constraint policy proved in window_kkt_projection_variance_note,
the 41 residual blocks have a common joint derivative-moment extension,
and the free KKT-times-derivative equations hold in the minimum-norm
extension. Thus adding only that U block and those products, without
further nonmonotone U constraints, cannot remove this exact point either.

Files and replay
----------------
cover-residual-psd-rational-projection.json stores the 464 rational moments,
90 exact updates, and the 41 LDL witnesses. Its driver is
cover_residual_psd_rational_projection.py.

cover-residual-psd-rational-inputs.json freezes the exact old row ingredients
and the complete model fingerprint; its driver regenerates ingredients
only and never changes the point.

Run from the repository root:
  python -S -B -X utf8 research-goal-20261016/cover_residual_psd_rational_replay.py
The successful receipt is cover-residual-psd-rational-replay.json.

The two SCS source/output pairs are retained byte-for-byte only as discovery
provenance. Their output files contain nonstandard JSON NaN fields in solver
diagnostics and must not be treated as strict mathematical certificates.
The new rational certificate files use finite integer or Fraction-string
values and are written with allow_nan=False. The independent replay only
reads the finite 464 discovery coordinates before redoing the exact
projection; every asserted inequality and PSD identity is then rational.
