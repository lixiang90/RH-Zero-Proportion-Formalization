Separated spectral slack: source audit and sharpness obstruction
2026-10-17

Status and scope
----------------
Static source audit and exact algebra only. No optimizer, parameter search,
Lean, new cover job, or frozen-source edit. The strict cap is a universal
consequence of separation; it is NOT an extra exclusion on positions.
This note proves no new zero-proportion bound and does not close the 9D floor.

1. Exact meaning and direction of the defect
------------------------------------------
GramDefect20261015/Core.lean:3295 defines
  Psi(t)=(t-1)^2                  if t<=2,
         2*t-3                  if t>2.
Core:3425 defines defect(G)=Tr Psi(G)=sum_j Psi(lambda_j(G)); :3430 defines
blockDefect by principal compression. This is a nonnegative spectral
deviation functional centred at eigenvalue ONE. It is neither the rank
deficit m-rank(G), nor 2I-G, nor a sum of 2-lambda_j.

Core:3363 stable_rank_trace_sharp and :3379 stable_rank_trace connect this
specific Psi to the trace/rank inequality. Core:4597 count_defect and the
actual WindowCountingBase.eventually_h7 retain this same defect. The tail
bridge has direction
  (H-epsilon)*N + Dcirc <= N0simple.
An improved counting bound therefore needs a stronger LOWER payment for D.
A smaller upper bound on D does not supply a positive counting bonus.

Core:3561 proves Psi convex, and :4841 pinching_partition uses convexity to
prove sum(blockDefect)<=global defect. Convexity, not operator monotonicity,
is the relevant property. Psi decreases on [0,1] and increases on [1,infinity).
For example (1/2)I<=I but Tr Psi((1/2)I)=m/4>0=Tr Psi(I). One cannot move
eigenvalues downward and infer a fixed direction for the defect by matrix order.

3. Sharp two-by-two and three-by-three obstructions
--------------------------------------------------
For any 0<r<=Lambda-1, take
  G=[[1,r],[r,1]].
This is a real PSD correlation matrix with eigenvalues 1-r,1+r<=Lambda<2.
Yet exactly
  defect(G)=2*r^2=offDiagSq(G).                         (2)
The rational choice r=1/2 already has lambda_max=3/2<Lambda and both
quantities equal 1/2. Hence the cap alone cannot justify
  defect>=(1+epsilon)*offDiagSq with epsilon>0,
or any strictly positive per-vertex additive bonus. G=I independently rules
out the latter. Taking G direct-sum [1] gives an exact three-by-three
counterexample with the same equality and triangle product zero. Larger
block sums give the same obstruction in every size.

These examples refute improvements inferred ONLY from PSD, diagonal one
and the strict cap; they are not claimed to be a new actual-kernel position
counterexample. In fact (1) is already an exact algebraic equality for any
unit-diagonal actual limiting kernel Gram whose cap is below two. Kernel-
specific improvements must therefore change which energy is lower-bounded
or use additional kernel/counting structure, not amplify its full pair sum.

4. What a cubic or higher spectral support could and could not add
----------------------------------------------------------------
For a unit-diagonal PSD G with spectrum in [0,Lambda], put X=G-I. Spectral
calculus gives the exact valid inequality
  0<=Tr[(Lambda*I-G)*X^2]
    =(Lambda-1)*defect(G)-Tr(X^3).                      (3)
Consequently a separately established positive triangle sum could imply
  defect(G)>=Tr(X^3)/(Lambda-1).
For Hermitian zero-diagonal X,
  Tr(X^3)=6*sum_(i<j<k) Re(G_ij*G_jk*G_ki).
The coefficient 1/(Lambda-1)>1 does NOT amplify the pair energy; it multiplies
a different signed triangle moment, which can vanish or be negative. The
two-by-two direct-sum example makes it vanish while pair energy is positive.
No positive triangle lower certificate has been produced here.

Likewise any polynomial p<=Psi on [0,Lambda] gives Tr p(G)<=defect(G), but
cannot exceed the exact identity (1) pointwise. It can help only if its
different moments admit a substantially easier global lower payment.
The existing StructuralResearch20261016/Fenchel.lean already formalizes
the matrix supporting-functional bound with dual spectrum <=2. When the
primal cap is Lambda<2, its exact quadratic optimizer S=2(G-I) already has
spectrum <=2*(Lambda-1)<2 and attains (1). Strict slack can ease construction
of an approximate dual, but does not increase the maximum pointwise payment.

5. Two different cutoff changes: neither is a free improvement
-------------------------------------------------------------
First, if one keeps the centre ONE and replaces the cutoff two by a<2,
the convex tangent extension is
  Phi_a(t)=(t-1)^2                                  for t<=a,
            2*(a-1)*t-(a^2-1)                      for t>a.
For 1<a<2 it is pointwise <=Psi. On a<t<=2 the loss is (t-a)^2; on t>2
the loss is (2-a)*(2*t-2-a)>=0. On blocks with spectrum <=a it is simply
equal to Psi again. Thus lowering this cutoff weakens the global functional
and adds nothing on the separated block. The direction is adverse.

Second, the actual imported rank_trace_mult parameter is NOT that fixed-
centre change. Sole-owner read of the pinned dependency
  .lake/packages/Zeta23/Zeta23/ZeroSide/RankTraceMult.lean:30
confirms exactly
  gc(a,t)=t^2-a*t-max(t-a,0)^2.
Its :281-284 rank_trace_mult theorem is
  a*Tr(P)+sum gc(a,lambda(P))+2*a*Tr(Q)-a^2*b
    <=||P+Q||_F^2,
for a>0 and posIndex(Q)<=b (with the stated positive factorization inputs).
The corresponding nonnegative shifted defect is
  Psi_a(t)=gc(a,t)+a^2/4
          =(t-a/2)^2-max(t-a,0)^2.                    (4)
For a=2 this is exactly the repository's Psi. For a<2 it is not ordered
against Psi globally: with a=19/10, Psi_a(1)=1/400>0=Psi(1), whereas
Psi_a(10)=6517/400<17=Psi(10). One may not retain the increased near-one
value but discard the changed trace coefficient and large-eigenvalue branch.

There is an exact leading-coefficient check for this parameter family.
Let r be the number of simple columns, Tr(P)<=r and 2*b<=N-r, precisely the
counting inequalities used in Core. Define D_a=Tr Psi_a(Gram) and
  d=a-a^2/4=1-z, z=(a-2)^2/4.
Rearranging the general rank-trace theorem gives
  d*r >= 2*a*Tr(A)-||A||_F^2-a^2*N/2+D_a.             (5)
In the normalized leading-coefficient model Tr(A)~N and
||A||_F^2~J*N, the baseline in (5) is (2*d-J)*N.

For Lambda<=a<=2 and a unit-diagonal separated block, all eigenvalues are
below a, so exactly D_a=offDiagSq+z*m. For a unit-diagonal two-point Gram
with correlation r0 in [0,1], its shifted defect is
  2*r0^2+2*z-max(1+r0-a,0)^2.
This is increasing in r0>=0. If the existing pair floor is c<=(a-1)^2,
it pays at least 2*c+2*z. Thus, in this ideal normalized matching model,
retaining the full +z per retained point yields the same existing energy
floor and pressure constants through
  kappa_a=(H-B-2*z)/(1-c-2*z).
This formula includes both the modified baseline and modified coefficient
of the counted simple points. Its difference from a=2 is exactly
  kappa_a-kappa_2
    =-2*z*[(1-c)-(H-B)]/[(1-c)*(1-c-2*z)].             (6)
For the present H,B,c, the bracket is positive and denominators are positive,
so every z>0 makes the ratio SMALLER. This is an algebraic normalized-family
comparison, not a newly formalized finite-height counting theorem; all its
finite-height/strip/pair perturbation consumers would still need proofs.

For scale only, at a=Lambda, z=1203409/4225000000. Using the existing exact
new-window H lower, B and c=.0088 as identical inputs in both formulas gives
  kappa_2 approximately .6736017669582564,
  kappa_Lambda approximately .6734140719546109,
a decrease of approximately .0001876950036454. Neither number here is an
unconditional new bound; the new-window global energy premise remains open.

Conclusion limited to this method
--------------------------------
The strict cap is preserved as a theorem upstream, then weakened before
matching. For the current Psi it gives no extra deficit beyond the EXACT
full quadratic already used. The pair-energy coefficient is sharp even
under a much smaller cap. The obvious general rank-trace cutoff change
loses leading counting coefficient after all terms are accounted for.
A genuinely different signed higher moment with an easier global lower
payment remains a possible research route, but no such payment is supplied
by the cap itself and none is asserted here.
