Statement, with precise conventions
----------------------------------
Let N>=2, 0<s<=1, g_r>=s for r=0,...,N-2, and
  p_0=0, p_i=sum_(r<i) g_r,
  F(g)=sum_r b_r*g_r + sum_(i<j) a_ij*w(p_j-p_i),
  B=sum_r b_r.
The proof works for any real function w at the displayed arguments and
arbitrary fixed real a,b. Actual applications retain their positive ledger
and actual kernel; no positivity, smoothness or phase relaxation of w is
used for the rounding comparison itself.

Assume g is a GLOBAL minimum on the entire separated orthant. For real
z>=1 define the actual-integer linear interpolant
  hatw(z)=(1-frac(z))*w(floor(z))+frac(z)*w(floor(z)+1).
At an integer, the coefficient of the second endpoint is exactly zero.
Write s_ij=p_j-p_i, d_ij=j-i, delta=1-s. Then

  sum_(i<j) a_ij*[w(s_ij)-hatw(s_ij+d_ij*delta)] <= delta*B.    (R)

The integer in w(n) denotes the real embedding of n. This is the same
actual w as in F. Old-window integer values cannot replace new-window
values. In the concrete actual24 energy, F is WindowGapEnergy.Fw24 and
B is NewLedger.W.B, not an unrelated energy-normalization constant.

Finite convex-combination proof; no probability integration needed
---------------------------------------------------------------
Set x_i=p_i-i*s. Then x_0=0 and
  x_(i+1)-x_i=g_i-s>=0.
For 0<=u<1 define integer positions
  q_i(u)=i+floor(x_i+u).
They satisfy q_0(u)=0 and
  q_(i+1)(u)-q_i(u)=1+floor(x_(i+1)+u)-floor(x_i+u)>=1>=s.
Thus their gap vectors belong to the ORIGINAL separated orthant for every
u. They need not obey the orientation g_0<=g_(N-2), a pressure-root box,
the original artificial cell, or a KKT contraction. None is required by
the global-minimum clause. In the actual Lean interface, use Relevant.2.2
and the feasible q gaps, not just directional_nonneg or local minimality.

Put n_i=floor(x_i), f_i=x_i-n_i in[0,1). For f_i>0 the sole jump in[0,1)
is at theta_i=1-f_i; for f_i=0 there is no jump in that half-open interval.
Sort the distinct internal theta_i and add endpoints0 and1:
  0=t_0<t_1<...<t_m=1.
There are at most N-1 internal thresholds because x_0=0, so m<=N.
Define lambda_l=t_(l+1)-t_l>0 and Q^l=q(t_l), l=0,...,m-1.
The floors are constant on each half-open interval[t_l,t_(l+1)); choosing
the LEFT endpoint includes its right-continuous jump correctly. Equal
thresholds are merged. No sample at u=1 is used. These are at most N
joint configurations with nonnegative REAL weights summing to1. We do not
claim the weights are rational for arbitrary real g.

The finite weighted average of floor(x_i+u) is n_i+f_i=x_i: the part of
[0,1) after its sole threshold has length f_i. Hence
  sum_l lambda_l*(Q^l_j-Q^l_i)=d_ij+(x_j-x_i)
                            =s_ij+d_ij*delta.             (1)
For h=x_j-x_i>=0 and v=frac(x_i+u) in[0,1),
  floor(x_j+u)-floor(x_i+u)=floor(h+v).
It therefore takes only floor(h), floor(h)+1; if h is an integer, only
the first value occurs. Adding the integer d_ij shows that Q^l_j-Q^l_i
takes the floor/ceiling of z=s_ij+d_ij*delta. By(1), the total weight
of the upper value is frac(z). Thus exactly
  sum_l lambda_l*w(Q^l_j-Q^l_i)=hatw(z).                  (2)
The different edges are correlated by the SAME u. This is essential for
their simultaneous feasible realizations, but causes no problem: only
linearity of a finite weighted sum is used, not independence of edges.

For an adjacent pair, (1) gives expected gap g_r+delta. Therefore
  sum_l lambda_l*F(gaps(Q^l))
    =sum_r b_r*g_r+delta*B+sum_(i<j) a_ij*hatw(z_ij).    (3)
Global minimality gives F(g)<=F(gaps(Q^l)) separately for every l. Multiply
by lambda_l and sum, use sum lambda=1, then cancel the pressure in(3).
This proves(R) with its stated <= sign. No Jensen inequality or assumed
convexity of w is involved. At zero fractional parts the formula remains
exact and has no division by a phase deviation.

What differs from the previous competitors and failed phase implication
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The inspected old cover_competitor_diagnostic.py uses g'=g+(p-n), with
integer gap vectors p,n, and optionally g'=p+sign*(g-n), sign=+/-1 selected
by parity. Its computations are floating diagnostics around ten old wells.
Translations preserve each gap's fractional part; these reflections retain
that deviation up to sign. Here each q gap is an integer and the total
span/pressure can change by a fractional amount. For example g=s*1 maps
to gaps1 for every u, a change delta=5/128 per gap, which is not an integer
translation. This is not a repackaging of that inspected move family.
The finite-global-comparison principle is the same general minimum
principle as WindowGlobalCompetitor, but the shared rounding map and its
exact marginal interpolation identity are additional mathematical content.
No exhaustive claim of historical priority outside the cited sources is made.

The old phase-lattice counterexample g=(9/4,1,...,1) refutes forcing every
span into a21/100 integer band from pressure plus unsigned A^2*sin^2 alone.
It is not a true sublevel/minimizer example; its actual F was above target.
It does not refute(R), which keeps the actual signed kernel before squaring
and subtracts its integer baseline. Nor does(R) itself prove those old69
basins complete. The previous exact pseudo-moment points only establish
feasibility of their listed polynomial outer constraints; this new actual
inequality was not among them. No evaluation of those points is made here.

Global relevance, falsifiable next gate and stopping boundary
-----------------------------------------------------------
The comparison theorem is uniform over the entire orthant, including all
faces, every current frontier and every old leaf. It does not depend on a
closed historical cell, a preferred basin, an arbitrary phase rank, or a
location-specific multiplier. It could supply one common additional
root/minimizer condition with a32-value integer baseline rather than a
new witness per old leaf. This is a possible change in proof description;
there is no measured leaf reduction or compliant source/runtime path yet.

Validity is established here, utility is not. To justify a later bounded
test, first express the proposed exclusion/classification implication with
the exact domain and target and the actual integer-interpolation upper
baseline. A single exact point in that domain satisfying its antecedents
but violating its claimed residual sign refutes that SUFFICIENT implication.
It does not refute(R) unless the point is also an actual global minimum.
Conversely, finding a low residual value or an old pseudo-point that passes
(R) shows that that test alone does not exclude it, not that the floor fails.
The condition may be weak because comparison configurations may have high
energy. No general improvement follows merely from having finitely many
rounding configurations.
