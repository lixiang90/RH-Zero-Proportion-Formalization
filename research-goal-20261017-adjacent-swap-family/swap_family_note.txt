The fixed complete family of eight adjacent-gap competitors
2026-10-17

This is a newly authorized natural finite family, separate from the
earlier frozen singleton swap(g5,g6).  Exactly all eight adjacent swaps
T_r, r=0,...,7, are fixed together.  No optimizer, direction selection,
parameter sweep, extra gap point, new Taylor table or Lean is used.

For any global minimum on the SEP orthant, ALL eight conditions
 F(T_r*g)-F(g)>=0
hold simultaneously because each permutation preserves the orthant.
This is not a statement that a selected single map lowers every parent
point.  The actual formal relevance predicate is
 StructuralGoal20261016.WindowMinimizerCover.Relevant,
defined in WindowMinimizerCover.lean as Orthant sep g, g0<=g8, AND
forall y, Orthant sep y -> Fw24 g<=Fw24 y.  The comparison domain is
the entire orthant, not only the ordered pressure root.  Therefore
competitors need not remain ordered or inside the artificial parent.

Each adjacent swap moves only its middle vertex.  Every term not incident
to that vertex cancels exactly.  At the one frozen parent midpoint, the
inherited actual-w degree23 integer Taylor table with the full order24
remainder gives strict signs for all eight differences:

 swap01: -0.000185542358
 swap12: +0.002087014682
 swap23: +0.000004379366
 swap34: -0.000000165571
 swap45: +0.000013706124
 swap56: +0.003017237394
 swap67: -0.004121499055
 swap78: -0.000216497077.

Exact rational intervals and all changed/cancelled terms are stored in
swap-family-static.json.  The calculation uses87 distinct scalar
arguments through existing integer-anchor tables and takes about.500s.
Independent stdlib direct-power interval evaluation reproduces all
eight cases.  There are four strict negative and four strict positive
differences.  The negative midpoint values indicate that fixed finite
competitor inequalities can carry useful global information, but they
do not exclude a pseudo-moment or any whole cell.

A degree<=4 upper polynomial R_r>=F(T_r*g)-F(g), valid throughout the
parent, would imply the necessary moment row E[R_r]>=0 at an actual
global minimizer.  This needs complete Taylor value/derivative error
payments on every transformed span's actual image interval.  No such
new moment model is built in this family diagnostic.  The separately
authorized fixed swap67 follow-up has its own source/directory and
does not alter this frozen eight-map diagnostic.
