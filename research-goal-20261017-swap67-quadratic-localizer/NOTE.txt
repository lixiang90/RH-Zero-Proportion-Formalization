Fixed67 quadratic upper-difference localizer
===========================================

Only the fixed swap of zero-based gaps 6 and 7 is considered, at the NEW
exact 714-moment point from the complete-eight-swap package. The old
parent case, profile, target, scalar tables, and all domains are unchanged.
There is no solver, scalar resampling, parameter scan, or Lean invocation.

The new necessary condition is not another scalar bound on the objective.
It retains a quadratic upper competitor difference Q2 and the position
correlations in the 10x10 localizer E[Q2*z*z^T], z=(1,y0,...,y8). This fits
within the existing degree-four shared moment representation.

Pointwise construction and validity
-----------------------------------
Take the 16 normalized-W scalar packets referenced by maps[6] of
swap-family-normalized.json.gz. For a transformed scalar use P4+E4; for
an original scalar use -(P4-E4). Each such signed scalar polynomial has
the exact form

    P(d)=c0+c1*d+c2*d^2+c3*d^3+c4*d^4,  |d|<=R.

Here all old/new coefficient errors and M5*R*d^4/120 payments have already
been included. In particular c4 can have either sign. Define

    q(d)=c0+c1*d+(c2+|c3|*R+max(c4,0)*R^2)*d^2.

Then q-P is nonnegative for every |d|<=R. Its cubic component is

    d^2*(|c3|*R-c3*d) >= 0.

For c4>=0, the quartic component is c4*d^2*(R^2-d^2)>=0. For c4<=0,
it is -c4*d^4>=0. These are exact scalar polynomial identities and domain
implications; no approximation or sampling is used. The source explicitly
checks each signed polynomial against its frozen packet and checks the
factorized-difference coefficient identity.

The weighted sum of q over the 16 signed scalar terms, together with the
EXACT pressure change, is Q2. All ledger weights are nonnegative. Thus

    Q2 >= D4upper >= Fw24(Tg)-Fw24(g)

on the original parent cell. The unchanged 33 nonzero edge terms have
already cancelled exactly. The actual Relevant predicate supplies global
minimum over the entire SEP orthant, so

    0 <= Fw24(Tg)-Fw24(g) <= D4upper <= Q2

at a relevant minimizer. T need not remain in the parent cell or in its
ordered subdomain; its original scalar envelopes are valid on the proved
whole image intervals.

For every affine function l(y), Q2*l(y)^2 is therefore nonnegative at
such a minimizer. Taking moments gives the necessary matrix inequality

    M1(Q2) = E[Q2*(1,y)*(1,y)^T] >= 0.

The degree is 2+1+1=4, so all its entries use the SAME 715 moments. A
scalar Q2 condition alone is weaker than scalar D4upper>=0. Nevertheless
this matrix involves affine test functions and is not supplied by the
eight scalar D4upper rows already checked in the frozen model. Whether it
separates a particular pseudo-moment is a separate exact/numerical question.

The one test and its limitation
-------------------------------
At the frozen new 714 point, the exact expectations, displayed here only
as decimals, are

    E[D4upper] approximately +0.000014487059480682778,
    E[Q2]      approximately +0.0063768584878346425.

The one authorized numpy.linalg.eigh of this fixed 10x10 exact-rational
matrix gave minimum eigenvalue hint +0.00028049510197924847 and maximum
hint +0.017108257086761308. Hence no integer-vector quantization was
attempted. There is no negative-vector certificate, no new solver, and
no cell exclusion. Positive floating eigenvalues are not claimed to be an
exact PSD proof or a joint-model exact feasibility proof.

The matrix, its rational hash, all signed reductions, final Q2, and its
difference from D4upper are saved in swap67-q2-localizer.json. An
independent stdlib replay, recorded separately when complete, checks the
coefficients and matrix entries without repeating the eigensolve. The
whole construction is conditional
on the same actual scalar envelopes as the frozen packets. Generic actual
global-competitor Lean logic is available, but no new concrete numerical
envelope or localizer instance is Lean-checked here.
