Complete adjacent-swap family in the fixed shared quartic model
==============================================================

Outcome and scope
-----------------
All eight adjacent swaps are used simultaneously at the single frozen
case r001000001000100110011001, SEP=123/128, target c=11/1250. No case,
window, pressure budget, target, anchor strategy, or solver settings were
selected by a scan. The new constraints strictly remove the prior exact
714-moment point: its swap(6,7) expectation is below -17/10000. However,
one authorized SCS call found a different numerical point, and the ONE
unadjusted rationalization of all its 714 coordinates, energy slack zero,
passes all 5803 rational rows and all 47 PSD blocks exactly.

An independent stdlib Fraction LDL replay also passes, comparing pivot
products to every stored integer Bareiss leading minor. Therefore this
particular fixed degree-four model, even with the complete eight scalar
global-competitor rows, has a strict positive-definite pseudo-moment
witness. It cannot exclude this cell. The witness is not an actual gap
vector, actual F value, actual KKT counterexample, or new RH proportion.
It does not show that all possible global competitors, their localizers,
higher-degree models, or other joint structures are ineffective.

Mathematical condition and shared representation
------------------------------------------------
For r=0,...,7, let T_r exchange actual gap coordinates r and r+1. Each
map preserves the whole SEP orthant. Relevant.2.2 in the actual
WindowMinimizerCover definition compares Fw24(g) with every point in
that orthant, including competitors outside the ordered half-domain or
the current cell. Thus all eight inequalities

    0 <= Fw24(T_r g)-Fw24(g)

hold simultaneously at every actual relevant minimizer. This global
property is stronger than local first- or second-order stationarity.

Only vertex r+1 moves. Exactly 68 changed positive-weight edge
occurrences remain across all eight maps: their counts in order are
9,8,9,8,8,9,8,9. All unchanged edge terms cancel BEFORE approximation.
The pressure change is (b_r-b_(r+1))*(g_(r+1)-g_r). Both energies use the
same nine normalized variables g=center+radius*y and all their shared
moments through degree four; no independent competitor variables occur.

For a left-incident edge (i,r+1), the transformed scalar span equals

    span(i,r)+g_(r+1) = span(i,r+2)-g_r.

For a right-incident edge (r+1,j), it equals

    g_r+span(r+2,j) = span(r,j)-g_(r+1).

The interval is the intersection of those two exact DBM-decomposition
intervals and the safe sum of the permuted adjacent gap boxes. Each
old/new scalar anchor is the deterministic midpoint of its resulting
whole interval; its radius is half the interval width. These operations
do not require optimization or actual function sampling.

For every changed scalar term, all zeroth through fourth coefficient
uncertainties and the fifth remainder are paid as in the frozen single67
note. With d=x-p and |d|<=R, P4=sum mu_j*d^j/j! and

    E4 = eps0+eps1*R
       + (eps2/2+eps3*R/6)*d^2
       + (eps4/24+M5*R/120)*d^4,

the enclosure is P4-E4<=w(x)<=P4+E4. The upper difference D_r uses
upper(new)-LOWER(old), nonnegative ledger weights, and the exact pressure
change. Thus Fw24(T_r g)-Fw24(g)<=D_r on the original parent cell, and
every relevant minimizer there satisfies D_r>=0. A lower difference would
not justify this constraint. Each new row is exactly

    -sum_nonconstant D_r[mon]*moment[mon] <= D_r[constant].

All eight are unsoftened. They have NO coefficient at energy-slack index
714. The original 5795 rows, the original 47 PSD blocks of sizes
55,10,...,10, all original geometry/KKT/sublevel conditions, and the sole
energy-slack objective are referenced byte-identically. Only eight scalar
rows are added, giving 5803 rows and still 714+one-slack variables.

Normalized coefficient source and formal limits
------------------------------------------------
The first preliminary static construction used the old w-prime table to
bound w5. Its three files swap_family_quartic_static.py,
swap-family-quartic.json.gz, and swap-family-quartic-build.json are retained
unchanged as provenance. No solver consumed them. A formal API audit
identified that the old w-prime coefficient intervals are not literal
copies of the normalized w-table derivatives. They remain explicit
analytic premises in old packages; they must not be described as newly
Lean-certified by table identity.

The sole consumed version is swap_family_normalized_static.py and
swap-family-normalized.json.gz. It uses ONLY the normalized actual-w
integer coefficient table for all new point jets and w5 bounds. Its
24 terms have degree23. Differentiating the coefficient polynomial five
times gives 19 terms of degree18, with coefficient factor j!/(j-5)! and
Taylor remainder M24*(1/2)^19/19!, M24=(44/7)^24. All used nearest integer
anchors lie in 1,...,32. There are 87 deduplicated scalar packets and 622
distinct old 1/64 grid cells. No new transcendental coefficient table or
actual scalar sampling position is added.

The normalized JSON coefficient table has separately been checked against
WindowJetTableData and the actual-profile/ledger literals. The existing
actual API WindowCellDerivative.interval supports q=5,d=18 once its
explicit finite check is supplied. Our exact rational Horner evaluation
does not perform the current FI-Q30 roundings. In particular its point
jet errors are not asserted to be the output of an existing FI point
checker without an additional check. A future pure exact-QI Horner bridge
can reuse interval addition/multiplication soundness, shifted_coeff, and
derivative_remainder. No concrete new 87-packet Lean instances have been
compiled here. The old base's actual-analytic premises also remain
explicit and unchanged.

The actual global-competitor logic itself is separately ordinary-Lean
checked in WindowGlobalCompetitor, with 11 standard-axiom roots. That
generic result supplies no particular numerical envelope or local
exclusion. Coefficient-source identity, exact rational arithmetic replay,
and completed actual Lean instantiation are distinct claims here.

-----------------------------------------

research-goal-20261017-swap-family-audit/independent_replay.py before the

The only rational candidate is Fraction(str(float(raw_primal[i]))) for
